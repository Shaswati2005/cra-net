\section{Summary}
AnatoPhaseNet classifies pathological gait from side-view binary silhouettes.
One sample is one gait cycle, represented by its 9 clinical key frames. The
model is a (2+1)D ResNet-18-style backbone (ImageNet-initialised), followed by a
body-part phase head, a posture-geometry branch, a severity head and a
multi-cycle walk head.

\section{Data and preprocessing}

\subsection{Dataset (GAIT-IT, side view)}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item 5 classes: normal, diplegic, hemiplegic, neuropathic, Parkinsonian.
\item 23 subjects, 813 walks (sequences), 3{,}813 gait cycles.
\item Cycles per class: normal 202, diplegic 846, hemiplegic 780, neuropathic 729, Parkinsonian 1{,}256.
\item Cycles per walk: 1 to 14 (median 4).
\item Severity labels from the sequence name: \texttt{lvl1} = mild (1{,}751 cycles),
      \texttt{lvl2} = severe (1{,}860), normal walks = normal (202).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{From a video to a cycle}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{Key frames.} GAIT-IT's \texttt{key\_frames.json} gives 9 frames per cycle:
  IC, LR, MSt, TSt, PSw, ISw, MSw, TSw and cycle completion (COMP).
  Consecutive cycles share their boundary frame when the file lists it twice.
\item \textbf{Phase jitter.} For every key frame, the frames at offsets $\{-2, 0, +2\}$
  are also stored (clamped to the sequence), giving 3 ``views'' per phase.
\item \textbf{Crop.} Pixels $>30$ define the bounding box, padded by 8\,\% of its height/width.
  The crop is resized, keeping its aspect ratio, so the long side is 96\,px, and pasted
  centred on a black $96\times96$ canvas (bilinear interpolation).
\item \textbf{Direction canonicalisation.} \texttt{\_back} walks are mirrored horizontally,
  so every cycle walks the same way (front and mirrored-back mean silhouettes correlate at 0.996).
\item \textbf{Cache.} Stored as \texttt{uint8} of shape $[3813, 3, 9, 96, 96]$
  (cycles, jitter views, phases, height, width), about 0.95\,GB.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Posture-geometry features (measured before cropping)}
Cropping makes every silhouette the same height, which hides crouch, stoop and vertical bob.
For each frame, 9 values are measured on the raw silhouette (threshold 127). All are divided
by the walk's median silhouette height $h_{\text{ref}}$, so camera distance cancels out:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}rlL@{}}
\toprule
\# & Feature & Definition \\
\midrule
0 & height & $h / h_{\text{ref}} - 1$ (crouch, stoop) \\
1 & width & bounding-box width $/ h_{\text{ref}}$ (stride width) \\
2 & lower-body width & width of the bottom 30\,\% of the box $/ h_{\text{ref}}$ (feet) \\
3 & upper-body width & width of the 15--55\,\% band $/ h_{\text{ref}}$ (arms and torso) \\
4 & head bob & (top $y$ $-$ walk median top $y$) $/ h_{\text{ref}}$ \\
5 & centroid height & (centroid $y$ $-$ top $y$) $/ h$ \\
6 & trunk lean & ($x$ of top 30\,\% $-$ $x$ of bottom 30\,\%) $/ h_{\text{ref}}$, signed in walking direction \\
7 & mass offset & (centroid $x$ $-$ box centre $x$) $/ h_{\text{ref}}$, signed in walking direction \\
8 & fill ratio & silhouette area $/ (h \cdot w)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
They are stored per cycle as $[3813, 3, 9, 9]$ (same jitter views as the images).
Empty frames give zeros; NaN/Inf values are set to 0.

\subsection{Network input}
The 9 frames $x \in [0,1]^{9\times96\times96}$ are turned into 3 channels
(no ImageNet mean/std normalisation):
\begin{align*}
\text{channel 1 (silhouette)} &= (x - 0.5)/0.5 \in [-1, 1],\\
\text{channel 2 (motion)} &= x_t - x_{t-1} \quad \text{(cyclic: phase 1 minus phase 9)},\\
\text{channel 3 (deviation)} &= x_t - \tfrac{1}{9}\textstyle\sum_{t'} x_{t'} \quad \text{(difference from the cycle's gait energy image)}.
\end{align*}
Tensor fed to the backbone: $[B, 3, 9, 96, 96]$.

\section{Architecture, layer by layer}

\subsection{Backbone: (2+1)D ResNet-18 (stem + layers 1--3, layer 4 removed)}
Each residual block (\emph{FactorizedSpatiotemporalBlock}):
\[
\begin{aligned}
u &= \mathrm{ReLU}(\mathrm{BN}(\mathrm{Conv}_{1\times3\times3}(h))) && \text{spatial}\\
u &= \mathrm{ReLU}\big(u + \mathrm{BN}_t(\mathrm{Conv}_{3\times1\times1}(\mathrm{circpad}(u)))\big) && \text{temporal, circular over the 9 phases}\\
u &= \mathrm{BN}(\mathrm{Conv}_{1\times3\times3}(u)) && \text{spatial}\\
\text{out} &= \mathrm{ReLU}(u + \text{shortcut}(h)) && \text{shortcut} = \mathrm{Conv}_{1\times1\times1}+\mathrm{BN} \text{ when shape changes}
\end{aligned}
\]
All convolutions have no bias. Temporal padding is \emph{circular} because a gait cycle is periodic
(COMP $\approx$ the next IC). $\mathrm{BN}_t$'s scale is initialised to 0, so at the start the
block is exactly the pretrained 2D ResNet block.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lLlr@{}}
\toprule
Stage & Operation & Output $[C, T, H, W]$ & Params \\
\midrule
Input & silhouette, motion, deviation & $3\times9\times96\times96$ & -- \\
Stem & Conv $1{\times}7{\times}7$, stride $(1,2,2)$, 64 ch; BN; ReLU & $64\times9\times48\times48$ & 9{,}536 \\
 & MaxPool $1{\times}3{\times}3$, stride $(1,2,2)$ & $64\times9\times24\times24$ & 0 \\
Layer 1 & 2 blocks, 64 ch, stride 1 & $64\times9\times24\times24$ & 172{,}800 \\
Layer 2 & 2 blocks, 128 ch, first block spatial stride 2 & $128\times9\times12\times12$ & 624{,}384 \\
Layer 3 & 2 blocks, 256 ch, first block spatial stride 2 & $256\times9\times6\times6$ & 2{,}493{,}952 \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{Backbone total (of which 517{,}888 are the new temporal convs + $\mathrm{BN}_t$)} & 3{,}300{,}672 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
The time axis is never down-sampled: all 9 phases survive to the head.

\subsection{Body-part phase head}
Input: $[B, 9, 256, 6, 6]$. $P = 7$ parts = 6 horizontal body strips + 1 global part.
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{Part pooling.} For each phase: average-pool and max-pool each of the 6 rows of the
  $6\times6$ map, plus the whole map; concatenate avg and max $\Rightarrow$ $7 \times 512$.
\item \textbf{Per-part projection.} A separate linear map per part, $512 \to 128$ (no bias, no activation).
\item \textbf{Phase embedding.} Add a learned vector per (phase, part), $9\times7\times128$.
\item \textbf{Temporal conv.} Grouped Conv1d (one group per part), kernel 3, circular padding,
  then BN and ReLU; added back as a residual.
\item \textbf{Three summaries over the 9 phases, per part} (each 128-d):
  \begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
  \item attention pooling: score$_t = f_t \cdot a_p$, weights $= \mathrm{softmax}$ over the 9 phases;
  \item max over phases;
  \item half-cycle symmetry: $\frac{1}{4}\sum_{t=1}^{4} |f_t - f_{t+4}|$ (one leg's stance vs.\ the other's).
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Descriptor.} $7 \times 384 = 2688$-d, BatchNorm1d.
\item \textbf{Per-part classifiers.} Dropout 0.3, then one linear $384 \to 5$ per part $\Rightarrow$ 7 sets of logits.
\end{enumerate}
Parameters: 832{,}419.

\subsection{Posture-geometry branch}
Input: $[B, 9, 9]$ (phases $\times$ features).
BatchNorm1d(9) $\to$ Conv1d $9\to64$, $k{=}3$, circular, BN, ReLU $\to$ residual Conv1d $64\to64$, $k{=}3$,
circular, BN, ReLU $\to$ [mean, max, half-cycle symmetry] over phases $= 192$-d $\to$ BatchNorm1d
$\to$ Dropout 0.3 $\to$ Linear $192\to5$. This is an 8th classifier.
Parameters: 15{,}639.

\subsection{Output heads}
Cycle descriptor $g = [\,2688 \text{ (image)}, 192 \text{ (geometry)}\,] = 2880$-d.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lLr@{}}

\midrule

large for irregular (e.g.\ Parkinsonian) strides and small for stereotyped ones.

\subsection{Activation and output functions}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lL@{}}
\toprule
Function & Where it is used \\
\midrule
ReLU & after every conv + BN in the backbone, the part-head temporal conv, both geometry convs,
  the cycle embedding, and the hidden layer of the walk head \\
Softmax & attention pooling over the 9 phases (per body part); turning logits into class
  probabilities at test time \\
Log-softmax & inside every loss; walk voting averages log-probabilities over the cycles of a walk \\

The network outputs raw logits. Prediction $= \arg\max$ of the logits, which is the same
as $\arg\max$ of the softmax.

\section{Training objective}
Focal loss with label smoothing ($\gamma = 1.5$, $\varepsilon = 0.05$, $C$ classes):
\[
\mathrm{FL}(z, y) = -\sum_{c=1}^{C} (1 - p_c)^{\gamma}\, \tilde y_c \log p_c,\qquad
p = \mathrm{softmax}(z),\quad
\tilde y_c = \begin{cases} 1-\varepsilon = 0.95 & c = y\\ \varepsilon/(C-1) = 0.0125 & c \ne y\end{cases}
\]
Total loss per step:
\[
\mathcal{L} = \underbrace{\mathrm{FL}(\bar z, y)}_{\text{cycle}}
+ 0.5\,\underbrace{\tfrac{1}{8}\textstyle\sum_{k=1}^{8}\mathrm{FL}(z_k, y)}_{\text{each part + geometry}}
+ 0.3\,\underbrace{\mathrm{CE}(s, y_{\text{sev}})}_{\text{severity}}
+ 1.0\,\underbrace{\mathrm{FL}(z_{\text{walk}}, y)}_{\text{walk head}}
\]
$\bar z$ is the mean of the 8 classifier logits. The severity term is plain cross-entropy
(no focal term, no smoothing) and skips cycles without a severity label. Padded cycle slots
in a walk are masked out of all losses.

\subsection{How a batch is built}\label{sec:batch}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0pt
\item Draw $K$ uniformly from $\{2,\dots,6\}$; take $\lfloor 16/K \rfloor$ walks.
\item Walks are sampled with replacement, with probability $\propto 1/\sqrt{n_c}$
  ($n_c$ = number of training walks of that class), so rare classes are seen more often
  without being forced to equal frequency.
\item From each walk take $K$ \emph{consecutive} cycles from a random start. Shorter walks are
  padded with a repeat of their first cycle and a mask.
\item For each of the 9 phases, one of the 3 jitter views is chosen at random.
\end{enumerate}

Not used: early stopping, mixup/cutmix, stochastic depth, subject-adversarial training
(tested separately: 95.16\,\%, no gain).

\subsection{Image augmentation (on the GPU, every training step)}\label{sec:aug}
The same transform is applied to all 9 frames of a cycle, so the motion between phases is unchanged.
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item Random affine: rotation $\pm5^\circ$, scale $1\pm0.08$, translation $\pm0.05$ in normalised
  coordinates (about $\pm2.4$\,px), bilinear, zero padding.
\item Segmentation noise: with probability 0.15 a $3\times3$ dilation, with probability 0.15 a
  $3\times3$ erosion, otherwise unchanged.
\item Random erasing: probability 0.2; box height and width $\in [6, 16]$\,px; same box in all 9 frames.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Initialisation}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item Backbone: 75 tensors from ImageNet ResNet-18 (\texttt{resnet18\_pretrained.pth}): stem, layers 1--3,
  all conv weights and BN parameters and running statistics. The 30 new tensors are the temporal
  convs and their $\mathrm{BN}_t$ (scale initialised to 0).
\item Part projections and part classifiers: Xavier uniform. Phase embeddings: $\mathcal N(0, 0.02^2)$.
  Attention vectors: 0 (uniform attention over phases at the start).
\item Everything else: PyTorch defaults (Kaiming uniform for conv and linear layers, BN scale 1 and shift 0).
\end{itemize}

\section{Evaluation protocol}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item The 23 subjects are shuffled (seed 42) and dealt into 10 folds (2--3 subjects each).
  Every person's cycles, of all five gait types, sit in one fold only.
\item For fold $i$: test $=$ fold $i$, validation $=$ fold $i{+}1$, training $=$ the other 8 folds.
  Validation is only monitored; it does not choose the checkpoint.
\item Test-time augmentation: the logits of the 3 jitter views are averaged.
\item Cycle accuracy: each cycle on its own. Walk accuracy: the walk head sees all cycles of a walk.
  The vote (mean log-probability over cycles) gives 95.53\,\%.
\end{itemize}
